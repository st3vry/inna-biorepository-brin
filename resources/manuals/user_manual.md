# Indonesian Nucleotide Archive (INNA) Bio Repository

## Account Registration

1. INNA Bio Repository can be accessed from [INNA Bio Repository](https://inna-prototype.brin.go.id/)
   ![Account Registration step 1](/manuals/access_step_1.png)
2. To **Register** you will redirected to BRIN SSO page, click **Daftar!** button to register.
   ![Account Registration step 2](/manuals/access_step_2.png)
3. Completely Fill the _Registrasi SSO BRIN_ form and click **Create User** button.
   ![Account Registration step 3](/manuals/access_step_3.png)
4. You can now login to INNA Bio Repository.

## BioProject

1. From INNA Bio Repository Dashboard click **My BioProject** menu.
   ![BioProject step 1](/manuals/bioproject_step_1.png)
2. To create new BioProject click **Create New BioProject** button.
   ![BioProject step 2](/manuals/bioproject_step_2.png)
3. Fill the required _Submitter Info_ Form, making sure the _Data Release_ form selected accordingly then click **Next** button.
   ![BioProject step 3](/manuals/bioproject_step_3.png)
4. Fill the required _General Info_ Form, then click **Next** button.
   ![BioProject step 4](/manuals/bioproject_step_4.png)
5. Fill the required _Project Type_ Form, then click **Next** button.
   ![BioProject step 5](/manuals/bioproject_step_5.png)
6. Fill the required _Target_ Form, to search the Organism you can click the form and search for the Organism. After you select the Organism, confirm with the **Select this Organism** button. Make sure all of the required form is filled then click **Next** button.
   ![BioProject step 6](/manuals/bioproject_step_6.png)
   ![BioProject step 7](/manuals/bioproject_step_7.png)
7. Fill the required _Publication_ Form, then click **Next** button.
   ![BioProject step 5](/manuals/bioproject_step_8.png)
8. Check all the information on _Review_ Form is correct, then click **Finish!** button.
   ![BioProject step 5](/manuals/bioproject_step_9.png)

## BioArchive Download Request

1. From the front page, choose **Collections** menu then choose **Bioarchive**.
   ![Bioarchive download request step 1](/manuals/biodl_step_1.png)
2. Select the bioarchive that you want to download.
   ![Bioarchive download request step 2](/manuals/biodl_step_2.png)
3. Confirm the bioarchive that you want to download by clicking **Request to Download** button.
   ![Bioarchive download request step 3](/manuals/biodl_step_3.png)
4. Completely fill the _Request a Permission_ form and click **Submit Request** button.
   ![Bioarchive download request step 4](/manuals/biodl_step_4.png)
5. If the request is approved the download button will appears on the bioarchive page.
   ![Bioarchive download request step 5](/manuals/biodl_step_5.png)
6. To download the file, choose the folder and click the **Download** button.
   ![Bioarchive download request step 6](/manuals/biodl_step_6.png)
